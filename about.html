A little about me

Curiosity, clarity,
and care.

I work at the intersection of people, product experiences, and technology—especially when there is an opportunity to make something easier to understand or more accessible.

Design is a way of thinking.

I'm a University of Michigan School of Information student on a UX Design pathway, with an interest in product management and human-centered AI.

My work ranges from presenting an accessibility assessment concept to a nonprofit client and Code for Good judges, to refining a mobile experience in Figma, to building and publishing websites.

What connects these projects is a focus on defining the real problem, telling a clear story, and being transparent about what has—and hasn't—been validated.
